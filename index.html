Picklist

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026 · Developer: Namaki Software Group · Package: com.namaki.picklist

Picklist is a fully offline shopping-list app for Android. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data we collect

None. Picklist does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. We have no servers and receive no information from the app.

Data stored on your device

  • Your lists, items, quantities, prices and optional currency label are stored only in the app's local storage on your device.
  • Your 4-digit PIN is never stored. Only a one-way (SHA-256) hash of it is kept in the device's secure storage.
  • This data never leaves your device through the app.

Internet, third parties and permissions

  • The app works without internet and makes no network requests.
  • No accounts, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The app requests no special Android permissions.
  • Tapping the privacy-policy link in Settings opens this page in your browser, outside the app.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset app (or Forgot PIN? on the lock screen), clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. All data is removed permanently. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, it cannot be recovered.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
